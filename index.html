Meet the Animals

Learn where they live, what they eat, and what makes them special.

Blue Whale

Where found: Oceans around the world.

Habitat: Open ocean, especially areas with lots of food.

Diet: Mostly krill.

Size: Up to about 30 meters long.

Fun fact: The blue whale is the largest animal known to have ever lived.

Dolphin

Where found: Oceans and some rivers around the world.

Habitat: Coastal waters and open oceans.

Diet: Fish, squid, and other small marine animals.

Size: Many species are around 2–4 meters long.

Fun fact: Dolphins use sounds and echolocation to find objects.

Great White Shark

Where found: Coastal waters in many parts of the world.

Habitat: Temperate and subtropical oceans.

Diet: Fish, rays, seals, and other marine animals.

Size: Can grow to around 6 meters.

Fun fact: Great white sharks have an excellent sense of smell.

Octopus

Where found: Oceans around the world.

Habitat: Rocky areas, coral reefs, and the ocean floor.

Diet: Crabs, shrimp, fish, and other small animals.

Size: Varies greatly between species.

Fun fact: Octopuses can change their color and skin texture to hide.

Jellyfish

Where found: Oceans worldwide.

Habitat: From coastal waters to the deep ocean.

Diet: Small fish, plankton, and tiny marine animals.

Size: Varies between species.

Fun fact: Jellyfish have existed for hundreds of millions of years.

Sea Turtle

Where found: Tropical and temperate oceans.

Habitat: Coastal waters, coral reefs, and open ocean.

Diet: Seagrass, jellyfish, algae, and small marine animals.

Fun fact: Female sea turtles usually return to beaches to lay their eggs.

Crab

Where found: Oceans, rivers, lakes, and coastal areas.

Habitat: Sandy beaches, rocky shores, coral reefs, and muddy sea floors.

Diet: Plants, algae, small animals, and dead organisms.

Fun fact: Crabs have hard outer shells that protect their bodies.

Clownfish

Where found: Warm waters of the Indian and Pacific Oceans.

Habitat: Coral reefs, usually living among sea anemones.

Diet: Algae, plankton, and small food particles.

Fun fact: Clownfish have a special relationship with sea anemones.
